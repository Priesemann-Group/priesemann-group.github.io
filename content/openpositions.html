Join Us

At any time, we welcome applications from creative researchers at all levels (Bachelor’s and Master’s thesis, PhD, PostDoc, team leader). Our aim is to understand learning and self-regulation (and their failure) in social and neural networks.

Information for all applicants

If our research topics resonate with your interest, please do not hesitate to send us your application.

Application documents

Please send a single PDF to application.priesemannATds.mpg.de, including:

  • Letter of motivation
  • CV
  • Transcript of records
  • Names of referees

Motivation letter

Your motivation letter (1–2 pages) should summarize your past research achievements, skills, and interests, and include a short outline (~½ page) of “your research interest” — the topic you would like to pursue in our group (this is not necessary for Bachelor and Master theses, but welcome).

If you provide this, I will have a careful look at your application. The short outline does not need to be a final research proposal; it serves as basis for us to see the potential fit, and to discuss with you potential research projects in an interview.

Use of AI

Please add a short statement to which degree you have been using “AI” to prepare your letter of motivation.

We encourage applicants from all backgrounds and genders to contact us.
