Join Us

At any time, we welcome applications from creative researchers of all nationalities and at all levels (Bachelor’s and Master’s theses, PhD, and postdoc). In Germany, it is common to apply to research groups even when no specific position is advertised. If our research closely matches your interests, please do not hesitate to send us your application.

How to apply

Application documents

Please send a single PDF to application.priesemannATds.mpg.de, including:

  • Short letter of motivation
  • CV
  • Transcript of records
  • Names of referees

Skills and use of AI

Please also include an informative statement about your programming, AI/ML, and/or analytical skills, and explain how you used AI tools to prepare your application.

Equal opportunity

We encourage applicants from all backgrounds and genders to contact us.

Degree programs in Göttingen

Interested in studying physics or data science in Göttingen? These degree programs offer routes from a broad undergraduate foundation to specialized, research-oriented Master’s study.

Physik+ (B.Sc.)

The four-year Bachelor “Physik Interdisziplinär” combines a strong foundation in physics with flexible interdisciplinary pathways such as artificial intelligence, philosophy, or sustainability.

Discover Physik+

Physics (B.Sc.)

The standard six-semester Bachelor of Science in Physics provides a broad foundation in experimental and theoretical physics.

Explore the Physics B.Sc.

Physics (M.Sc./Joint Degree)

The research-oriented Master of Science in Physics offers advanced study and specialization across Göttingen’s broad physics research landscape.

Explore the Physics M.Sc.

Applied Data Science (M.Sc.)

We also recommend this program to German and international students. It can be studied without German, and a student job may help fund your studies.

Usual application deadlines: 1 May and 1 November each year.
