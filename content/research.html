Research

Our research focuses on the theory of complex systems, from information processing in neural networks to disease spread in population networks. We combine the study of learning and self-organization principles in these two types of networks with approaches from statistical physics and information theory. We thereby aim to improve the working of biologically inspired artificial neural networks on the one hand, and to face future pandemics and similar health crises on the other hand.
