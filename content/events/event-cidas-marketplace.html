CIDAS Marketplace for Theses and Project Work June 18, 2026

A poster-session marketplace where students can discover thesis and project topics related to data science.

The CIDAS Marketplace for Theses and Project Work will take place in the foyer of the Göttingen Computing Centre/GWDG. Starting at 16:00, lecturers and researchers will present possible thesis and project topics related to data science in the form of a poster session.

The event is aimed at students of data science, computer science, and statistics, as well as everyone interested in data science from application-oriented disciplines.

The marketplace also offers an opportunity to learn about research from other groups and to exchange ideas with colleagues from the Göttingen Campus.

Time: from 16:00
Location: Foyer of the Göttingen Computing Centre/GWDG

Research groups interested in participating are asked to register by email by June 12, 2026. Please include the title of the poster or topic, the institution, and contact details.

More info: University of Göttingen event page
Contact: cidas.marketplace@uni-goettingen.de
