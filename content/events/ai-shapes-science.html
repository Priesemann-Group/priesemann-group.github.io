AI Shapes Science

14–15 January 2027

This focus workshop explores how AI will reshape daily scientific work and the way knowledge is aggregated, verified, and shared. Which changes could make the scientific system more resilient to what's coming? What ethical guidelines should govern the use of AI in research? How should AI be designed to serve the greater good of science and society?

Organizers: Andreas Schneider and Viola Priesemann
