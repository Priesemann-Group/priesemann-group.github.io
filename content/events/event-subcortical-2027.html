Subcortical Sensory Circuits 2027 Feb 9–12, 2027

International meeting on subcortical sensory circuits, including visual, auditory, somatosensory, multisensory, and computational perspectives.

The meeting Subcortical Sensory Circuits: visual, auditory, somatosensory, and beyond will take place from February 9–12, 2027 at SISSA in Trieste, Italy.

The meeting brings together researchers investigating subcortical circuits involved in perception and behaviour. Its scope includes experimental and computational approaches to understanding how subcortical structures integrate sensory signals with context and internal state.

Topics include motor circuits, visual circuits, auditory circuits, state modulation, computational views, and multisensory integration.

Time: February 9–12, 2027
Location: Aula Magna, SISSA, Trieste, Italy
Keynote: Viola Priesemann, February 11, 2027
